Welcome to the Lloyd Center Fan Page!

Hi! My name's oatsandsugar, and this is my fan page for Lloyd Center — Portland, Oregon's gloriously weird, half-empty, endlessly surprising indoor mall. I started this site because I wanted somewhere to document all the amazing little shops and spaces that have taken root in Lloyd Center over the past few years, before it all goes away.

If you don't know: Lloyd Center is closing later this year. It's being demolished and redeveloped. And look, I get it — the mall has been struggling for a long time. But something genuinely beautiful happened in its final years. As the big chains left and the rents dropped, a wave of incredible independent shops moved in: comic stores, tabletop gaming shops, a pinball museum, a record store, an actual lightsaber forge. The half-empty corridors turned into this accidental arts district that feels like nowhere else.

Me & Indigo at the Mall

My baby Indigo and I started visiting Lloyd Center regularly, and it quickly became one of our favorite places to go. There's something magical about wandering a mostly-empty mall with a baby in a stroller. The wide corridors are perfect for walks. The light comes in through the skylights. And around every corner there's another little shop run by someone who genuinely loves what they do.

Indigo loves looking at the bright colors in the comic shop, watching people play cards at Gambit's, and staring at the pinball machines at Star Tropics. I love that these places exist at all — that in the dying light of American mall culture, this little ecosystem of weird, wonderful, independent businesses found a home.

This site is a love letter to that. Every page is about a place we've visited and why it matters.
