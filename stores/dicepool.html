dicepool.com
Instagram:
@dice.pool

I went to dicepool expecting D&D and Pathfinder. I'm a tabletop gaming lover, and that's what I know. Indigo immediately found a little goblin mini and couldn't let it go. I can't wait to start painting minis with her.

But it was my partner who changed the trip. She showed me something I hadn't seen before: single and multi-player RPGs where you roll dice or draw cards, your character has an "event", and then you journal about it. I was skeptical. Part of the fun of D&D is playing to and with your friends. But I tried my first one, a game about being a lab worker in an (of course) freaky lab. And I had some of the most fun creative writing I've had in years.

My next website will definitely be publishing my RPG journals. What a blast.

One of the single-player RPGs we bought from there is literally set in an abandoned Lloyd Center. Perfection.

It was also where my partner and I found a collaborative mapmaking RPG and played our first game together.
