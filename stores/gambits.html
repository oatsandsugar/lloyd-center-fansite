Gambit's Games and Anime

Type:
Trading cards, games, and anime merchandise
Website:
gambits-games-and-anime.square.site
Instagram:
@gambitscards13

Gambit's has games, minis, plushies, Gundams, and a bunch more. And of course, snacks and tabletops to play on.

The first time we went, people were playing Marvel Dice Throne. We walked Indigo around and she saw how much fun people were having with tabletop gaming.

The second time we came, there was a Beyblade tournament going on. And people were costumed. One obvious champion in floor-length velvet, and (if I remember correctly) a gold-plated Beyblade necklace, invited my partner and I to have a spin. I tried my best, literally missed the battlefield once, and lost pretty quickly in my second round. My partner, in a prison-yard kind of move, challenged the velvet Beyblader and defeated them on her second attempt. We obviously left on a high note.
