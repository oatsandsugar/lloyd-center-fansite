Docking Bay 45 / Legion Sabers

Type:
Star Wars experience & lightsaber forge
Operated by:
Legion Sabers (Matt Morrison), Luminous Movement (Izzy Del Rio), Scum & Villainy Acquisitions
Size:
3,000 sq ft (formerly a mall storage unit)
Opened:
May 4, 2025
Hours:
Fri–Mon, 11am–7pm
Website:
legionsabers.com
Instagram:
@dockingbay45 · @legion_sabers

One of the most engrossing sci-fi spaces I've seen, and it was built by volunteers. So many "wows" from the baby, especially seeing the life-size Chewie.

They have a martial arts academy, a prop and cosplay shop, a lightsaber store. That's a cool combo. They run lightsaber battles. I had never swung a lightsaber at full strength before. What an experience.
