Docking Bay 45 / Legion Sabers

Type:
Star Wars experience & lightsaber forge
Operated by:
Legion Sabers (Matt Morrison), Luminous Movement (Izzy Del Rio), Scum & Villainy Acquisitions
Size:
3,000 sq ft (formerly a mall storage unit)
Opened:
May 4, 2025
Hours:
Fri–Mon, 11am–7pm
Website:
legionsabers.com
Instagram:
@dockingbay45 · @legion_sabers

A 3,000 square foot Star Wars experience, built by Portland community volunteers for less than $2,000. Props by Jamie Dull ("The Outer Rim") include a Millennium Falcon corridor, Emperor Palpatine figure, speaking Jawas, and C-3PO.

What's Inside

  • 150+ lightsaber hilt variations — replicas from the films and shows, $50 (basic) to $225+ (pixel sabers)
  • Lightsaber classes via Luminous Movement (family, longsword, rapier, eskrima)
  • Handmade cosplay helmets and props from Scum & Villainy Acquisitions
  • Immersive environment for photos
