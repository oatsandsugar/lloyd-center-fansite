Star Tropics Pinball Museum

Type:
Pinball arcade / museum
Owner:
Jason Andersen
Admission:
$8 all-day wristband, free play on all museum machines
Machines:
~11–12, rotating lineup
Hours:
Fri–Sat 12–7pm, Sun 12–5pm
Location:
2nd floor, next to Brickdiculous
Website:
startropicsarcade.com
Instagram:
@startropicspinballmuseum

When we arrived, there was a little rave going on, performed by a DJ who looked right out of an early 2000s emo image.

Incredible machines, including a dissected one that shows you how they work. They'll let you walk around to have a look without paying, but the games are well worth the cost of entry.
