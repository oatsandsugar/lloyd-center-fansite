Floating World Comics

Type:
Independent comic shop & publisher
Owner:
Jason Leivian
Founded:
2006
At Lloyd Center since:
August 2022 (moved from Old Town Chinatown)
Hours:
Mon–Sat 11am–7pm, Sun 11am–6pm
Website:
floatingworldcomics.com
Instagram:
@floatingworldcomics

Just one of the most incredible comic shops I have ever seen. Moebius comics I had never seen before, even Moebius collectable cards (which I totally bought). Toys like you've never seen, many seemingly handmade, or vintage Japanese plastic. Zines. Local work. All kinds of beautiful comics, and super knowledgeable staff.
