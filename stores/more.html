More Cool Spots

There's more to Lloyd Center than just the stores with their own pages.

Brickdiculous Shop & Gallery

An independent LEGO store. They buy, sell, and trade LEGO: new sets, used sets (branded "Played Well"), retired and limited sets ("The Vault"), custom minifigures, and bulk bricks. They also have a rotating gallery of builds from local LEGO artists, and run birthday parties and day camps.

Hours: Tue–Sat 11am–6pm, Sun 11am–5pm, closed Mon.

brickdiculous.com · @brickdiculoushq

Synth Library Portland

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit synthesizer lending library. Members can borrow synthesizers, drum machines, sequencers, and other electronic music gear for three-week periods. Drop-in hours every Thursday, 4–7pm. They also host workshops, jam sessions, and "Open Channels" monthly gatherings.

Founded in 2016 as part of S1 Portland. Moved to Lloyd Center in April 2024.

synthlibraryportland.org · @synthlibraryportland

Musique Plastique

A post-punk, electronic, and world music record shop run by Tony Remple since 2015. Musique Plastique was one of the first indie shops to move into Lloyd Center in June 2022, helping catalyze the wave of others that followed. They moved out of Lloyd Center in mid-2023 and are now at 3059 NE Glisan St.

musique-plastique.com · @musiqueplastique

The Card Vendors

Pop-up trading card vendors who set up shop around the mall. Location varies, hours are unpredictable. No website, no socials. You just have to be there.
